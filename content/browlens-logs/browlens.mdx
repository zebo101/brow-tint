Browlens brings eyebrow shape analysis and photo previews together in one brow app.

## Explore the full editor

The [homepage](/#brow-app) includes automatic face and brow landmark detection, guide lines for brow heads, arches, and tails, six draggable control points, and adjustments for thickness, length, angle, spacing, height, and arch height. A report compares arch-height and brow-length differences.

## Try the filter

The [eyebrow filter](/filter) handles placement automatically so you can choose a shape, inspect a live outline, and generate an AI photo preview. Compare Before on the left with After on the right, download the result, or try another shape.

Local analysis remains free without sign-up. AI photo generation keeps the existing sign-in and credit requirements. The site is available in English, Chinese, Korean, Japanese, German, Spanish, Italian, and Portuguese.

Start with the [eyebrow mapping guide](/blog/eyebrow-mapping-guide) or [eyebrow shapes guide](/blog/eyebrow-shapes-guide).
