---
title: About Browlens
description: An independent, self-funded brow app for mapping, editing, and previewing eyebrow shapes on your own photo.
---

Browlens is an AI eyebrow shape analyzer and virtual try-on tool built by an independent developer. It maps your natural brow geometry with six adjustable control points, compares left-right symmetry, and lets you preview different eyebrow styles on your own photo. You can adjust thickness, length, height, spacing, and angle to explore what naturally feels right for you.

The idea came from a simple frustration: every brow-shape guide online asks you to hold a ruler to your face and follow abstract rules. Browlens replaces the ruler with AI: you upload a photo, the tool maps your brows, and you can see how different shapes look on _your_ face before touching a tweezer. The aim is to make brow decisions easier to understand, with a visual starting point you can adjust yourself.

Brow mapping, editing, and analysis are free with no sign-up required. AI filter generation uses credits. The service is fully self-funded and built for people who want better brow decisions without booking a salon appointment. Whether you prefer a subtle change or want to explore a different arch, Browlens gives you space to experiment with your own photo, at your own pace, before deciding what to try.
