## Meet the brow app

Browlens helps you understand and explore your eyebrow shape on a photo. AI detects facial and eyebrow landmarks, and the full editor adds head, arch, and tail guide lines.

Drag six control points to correct placement. Adjust thickness, length, angle, spacing, height, and arch height, then review the differences in arch height and brow length between the two sides. Confirm your placement, choose a style, and generate an AI photo preview.

## Photo guidelines

Use a clear, front-facing photo of one person with both eyes open and both brows visible. Avoid hard shadows and hair or accessories covering the brows. Supported formats are JPG, PNG, and WebP, up to 15 MB.

## Local analysis and generated photos

Analysis and contour editing run on your device and do not require sign-up. Images are uploaded when you choose to generate an AI photo preview. Photo generation requires sign-in and credits, and takes time; it is separate from the instant local outline.
